A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clearer answers.

Something else on your mind?
Let’s have a conversation ↗

Which AC is right for my room?+

Floor area is a starting point. Sun exposure, ceiling height, occupancy, usage and installation conditions also matter. Share your room details so the options can be discussed properly.

Can you help with commercial cooling?+

You can enquire about cassette, ducted and VRF systems for offices, retail and other commercial spaces. Share the floor plan, intended use, location and project timeline to start a scope discussion.

What should my installation quote include?+

Ask for the exact model, equipment price, installation scope, materials, additional work, applicable taxes, warranty and payment terms. Confirm the full scope and schedule before committing.

What does a maintenance plan cover?+

Coverage depends on the agreed plan. Confirm visit frequency, included work, exclusions, spare-part charges, response arrangements and the list of covered units before enrolment.

Does the enquiry form book an appointment?+

No. It prepares a message that you review and send in WhatsApp. An appointment is confirmed only when the CRS Aircon team responds and agrees the details with you.
