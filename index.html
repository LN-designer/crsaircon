02 · How cooling works

See what happens inside your AC.

A split AC moves heat from inside your room to the air outside. Choose a part of the system, or let it play through the cycle. It is general knowledge, shown simply.

Step 1 of 5 · Absorb

The indoor coil takes heat from your room.

A fan pulls warm room air across a cold coil. The refrigerant inside absorbs the heat, and the cooled air is sent back into the room.

Worth asking

Where will the indoor unit sit, and will its airflow reach the places you actually use?

Warm, high-pressure Cool, low-pressure

Why installation details matter.

  • PipingCopper pipes, insulation and neat routing affect how well the system runs.
  • Vacuum & gasAir and moisture are removed from the lines before the gas is charged.
  • Drain slopeCondensate needs a continuous downward path out of the building.
  • Outdoor unitClear airflow and sturdy mounting. Coastal air is worth planning for.

    09 · Questions

    Good questions. Clearer answers.

    Something else on your mind? Call +91 98400 73436

    Which AC is right for my room?

    Floor area is a starting point. Sun exposure, ceiling height, occupancy, equipment and installation conditions also matter. Use the space planner for a starting list, then share your room details so CRS can check them properly.

    Can you help with commercial cooling?

    Yes. CRS supplies cassette, ductable and VRF systems and takes on commercial HVAC projects. Share the floor plan, intended use, location and timeline to start a scope discussion.

    What does the ₹999 AMC include?

    As published by CRS: two preventive services a year, gas pressure and leak checks, electrical checks, deep filter and condenser-coil cleaning, and priority breakdown support. Please confirm current terms, covered units and exclusions before enrolling.

    What should my installation quote include?

    Ask for the exact model, equipment price, installation scope, materials, additional work, applicable taxes, warranty and payment terms. The checklist above is a handy way to confirm each item.

    Do you repair and refill gas for all brands?

    CRS offers repair, gas refilling and spare parts for the brands it works with. Call or message with your AC’s brand and model so the team can confirm what is possible.

    Can I exchange my old AC?

    CRS runs an exchange offer: your old AC may have exchange value toward a new inverter AC, including Mitsubishi Electric models. The value depends on your old unit and is confirmed by the team when you enquire. No figure is quoted on this page.

    Does the enquiry form book an appointment?

    No. It prepares a message that you review and send yourself, in WhatsApp or by email. An appointment exists only when the CRS team replies and agrees the details with you.
